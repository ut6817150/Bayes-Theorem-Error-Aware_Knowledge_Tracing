# Bayes Annotation Codebook
Instructions for annotating participant transcripts from the Bayes reasoning study. Written to be followed by a human annotator or an LLM rater with no other context. The unit of annotation is one row, one participant answering one question. Each row carries one question_correct cell, five KC cells, and five misconception cells.

## 1. The task and the two passes
Annotation proceeds in two passes. Pass one, fill question_correct and the KC cells for every row of a question before opening any misconception cell. Pass two, fill the misconception cells. Never decide a correctness cell with a misconception decision in view, and never decide a misconception cell before that row's correctness cells are fixed.
Grade only what is in the transcript. The participant explained their reasoning aloud with unlimited preparation time and pen and paper available. The written sheets are not available to you, the transcript is the whole record.

## 2. Label values
- **question_correct:** correct, wrong, or NA (warm-up row only). Judged as the stated final answer against the question's gold key in section 6. This is the only cell where execution accuracy matters.
- **KC cells (kc1 to kc5):** correct, wrong, or NA. A KC cell is judged at the concept level, correct means that concept was soundly applied in the stated reasoning. An arithmetic or transcription slip on top of a sound application does not make a KC cell wrong, execution lives only in question_correct. NA means the concept was not engaged on this turn.
- **Misconception cells:** fired, quiet, NA, or unreadable. Fired means the misconception's signature conception is present in the reasoning. Quiet means the trap was set and the participant stepped past it. NA means the trap was not set on this turn. Unreadable means the trap was set but the explanation is too thin to adjudicate, when in doubt between fired and unreadable, choose unreadable.
Cells arrive pre-filled with NA wherever the question does not engage that KC or set that trap. For the models, the designed map's cells are the prediction targets, restricted to cells realizable on correct canonical routes, and every adaptively created cell or off-schedule fire is update-only, observed after that turn's predictions are locked, so annotate adaptive cells with full care, they move beliefs, they are never scored as targets. Do not change a pre-filled NA except under the adaptive engagement rule (3.2) or the Q6 KC3 rule (6, Question 6).

## 3. Universal rules, applied everywhere

### 3.1 Concept-level correctness
Each KC answers only for unsound acts of its own conceptual content. Classify every act in the transcript by what it is, not by where it sits in the solution. An upstream failure is charged to the upstream KC once, a KC whose own acts are sound is never charged for downstream incoherence built from a corrupted input, and a KC whose act never occurs because an upstream input was never produced is judged on whatever acts of its content do appear, or left NA if none do.
The readout-slip excuse, a stated operation whose reported result is wrong stays a slip, charged to the answer alone, only when two conditions hold, the construction's roles are sound on the page, every term labelled as what it is, and the reported value is traceable to one of the participant's own computed quantities, a stale intermediate, a mislabelled real value. Where the roles are scrambled, or the reported value is an orphan traceable to nothing the participant computed, the announced operation fails as an attempt, wrong on its KC.
Required-but-absent charges wrong only where a determinate attempt at the task exists. A transcript that never assembles any adjudicable act of any concept's content engages nothing, garble is below the act threshold everywhere, though a committed stated value is still graded on the answer channel.

### 3.2 Adaptive engagement, and attempt versus absence
NA is for transcripts where no attempt at the KC's task exists, wrong is for attempts that fail, including failures at the identification step, and engagement is established by the attempt, not by its success. A bare assertion with no constructed quantity, no comparison, and no reasoning engages nothing.

### 3.2.1 Method-relative engagement
Which KCs a turn engages follows the participant's actual method, not only the designed map. If the participant uses a valid, complete method for the posed problem that genuinely does not require a KC, that cell is NA, not wrong. If their method did require a step and the step is absent or unsound, the cell is wrong. A trap whose demand the participant's valid route never encounters is NA rather than quiet.

### 3.3 The licensure principle
Participants may only be required to use information stated in the question. No unstated world knowledge, stereotype, or link may be demanded of them, and importing unstated quantitative commitments is itself unsound. Where stated information is silent on a quantity, the licensed value is the null one, an unstated likelihood ratio is one, and the licensed update is no update.

### 3.4 The misread standard
Correctness is graded against the question as posed. A misread that is itself one of the catalogued conceptions, for example reading the two conditional directions as one thing, is graded and flagged normally. A true transcription misread, a wrong number picked up, a clause missed, makes wrong exactly the cells whose reasoning consumed the misread quantity, cells independent of it are graded on their merits, and no misconception fires, a misread is not a conception. Solving a different problem correctly is never converted to NA.

### 3.5 Misconception rules
Fires key on the conception in the reasoning, never on the answer token alone. A canonical wrong number with reasoning that does not show the conception is not a fire. Quiet under a set trap is positive evidence and must be an affirmative judgement, the conception is absent, not merely undiscussed. Ambiguous explanations resolve to unreadable. One turn can fire multiple misconceptions when the reasoning genuinely exhibits each conception, two real failures, two cells. The pair question_correct = correct with a fired misconception is legal, right answer, flagged reasoning.
Two kinds of traps govern where quiet can be written. Demand-traps are set by a computational step, denominator_neglect is the paradigm, and applicability follows the participant's method, a valid route that never demands the vulnerable step makes the cell NA. Temptation-traps are set by the stimulus itself, Q1's base-rate trap is the paradigm, the seductive description is present on every route, so applicability is universal on that turn.
Sole-production fires only where the posed updating task survives the participant's construal, a construal that dissolves the task carries its failure at the identification cells, the flag quiet.
Computational-flag fires require the pool-free construction built or the slot's value defended as a stated principle, a wrong given grabbed amid displayed ignorance is machinery failure, charged at the KC cells, quiet on the flag, conception, not incompetence.

A fire requires the conception manifest, not an act performed, so a fired flag may sit beside its home KC in any state, wrong when the conception drove an unsound act, NA when it manifested in words alone with no act to grade, and correct in principle when a sound act coexists with the articulated conception elsewhere in the turn. The rule cuts both ways, an NA or quiet cell is never evidence against a fire, and a sound act elsewhere never vetoes one, conception and competence can coexist.

Conjunction quiets follow the behavioral bar, the ranking is the conception's whole content, so a committed non-fallacious ranking in an entered habitat is the conception failing to manifest, no containment articulation demanded, and a construal that replaces the conjunction before ranking closes the habitat, not-applicable. Base-rate quiets on joint-product turns follow the articulation carve-out, a joint computed by the product rule faces no prior-versus-evidence judgment, the habitat closed for silent multipliers, not-applicable, unless the transcript makes the posterior-direction judgment in words, the prior's pull on the estimate reasoned aloud, which earns the articulated-weighing quiet independent of the machinery that follows.

The applied class extends to the denominator flag, a correct value supplied where the participant's own method demands the evidence slot is an applied quiet, the neglecter's counterfactual making the correctness informative, a wrong value in the same slot charging kc4 with the conception tested under the conception-not-incompetence rule.

Trap-agnostic observation semantics. Every flag cell in the dataset carries one three-way logic, regardless of whether a trap is scheduled on the turn. Fired where the conception is manifest, built, defended, or articulated. Quiet where the habitat was genuinely entered, the participant's own displayed method demanding the pool or facing the prior-weighing judgment, and the conception's negation displayed, the pool built, the total used as the divisor, the prior weighed or applied so that it scales the judgment, silence never qualifying. Not-applicable where the habitat never opened, the construal, collapse, or route closing it, and also where the transcript withholds all material for any adjudication, a measurement void, noted as such in the rationale. The unreadable token is retired, the observation logic pure three-way, fired, quiet, not-applicable, and designed cells voided by measurement failure enter the exclusion log as measurement failures rather than construal closures, preserving the instrument's failure statistic tokenlessly. The trap map retains exactly two roles, defining the pre-registered prediction targets and the Stage A reliability set, the observation stream itself is trap-agnostic and every cell is update-eligible.

A trap cell reads fired on manifestation, quiet only where the conception's habitat was reached, the pool demanded by the participant's own held task, the prior's relevance live in a judgment actually faced, and the conception affirmatively failed to appear. Where the participant's construal, collapse, or truncation closed the habitat before it opened, the cell reads not-applicable, no opportunity, no evidence, and the melt removes it before the models, quiet is evidence of absence, never absence of evidence.

Fires and quiets obey an asymmetric rule. Quiet may be written only where the trap is set, resistance needs something to resist, and unreadable likewise exists only on trap turns, it marks an owed observation the transcript withholds. Fired may be written on any turn where the flag's home KC is engaged, designed or adaptive, whenever the conception affirmatively manifests in the reasoning, overwriting the pre-filled NA, a fire needs no trap, only a mouth. On a trap-free engaged turn the cell therefore has exactly two states, fired or NA, an ambiguous transcript that merely fails to rule the conception out stays NA. Each question's flag pass ends with a full-catalogue sweep of every transcript for off-schedule fires, logged in one line even when it finds none.

### 3.6 The construal rule
Identification of the target quantity is graded against the posed text, execution of machinery is graded under the participant's own construal. A mistranslation is charged to a KC only when the distinction lost is that KC's own conceptual content, joint versus conditional to kc3, direction of conditioning to kc2, event structure to kc1. Everything downstream of the mistranslation is graded under the participant's construal, so sound machinery run on the misconstrued quantity earns its KC's credit. A mistranslation whose lost distinction is not any KC's content, a garbled number, a swapped event label, is a slip.
A declared construal is honoured where it fills a gap the stem or licensed knowledge leaves open, or anchors in ambiguous stated wording. A declared mechanism that contradicts stated or entailed structure is charged at kc1, the shield lapsing, and machinery operating inside a stem-contradicting world yields no evidence about any concept, its acts formally faithful to a structure that is not there, all machinery cells not engaged, the failure charged at its real homes, the world and the identification, never triple-priced.

A declared construal is honoured, an undeclared one is charged. Where a participant states a nonstandard reading or assumption aloud, anchored in the item's words or named as an assumption, execution is graded under it, but a reconstruction that would rationalise the transcript, an unstated twelve-sided die, an unstated replacement, is not supplied on the participant's behalf, the acts on the page are graded against the posed world.
The faithful-restatement test protects genuinely linguistic cases, and extends to verbal label-swaps, where a participant's labels for the posed quantities are exchanged but the content is coherent and sound under the swap, the swap is a fluency artifact, graded on the content. If the participant's own restatement of the event is faithful and the formalisation then loses the distinction, the sentence was comprehended and the mathematics dropped it, conceptual, charged. If the restatement itself is garbled in a way suggesting the sentence never landed, linguistic, slip, uncharged, logged with a language note. Note the demarcation, construing a posed joint as a conditional is kc3 content, the inverse fallacy is a different conception, swapping which way a conditional points, the two must not blur.

### 3.7 Act boundaries between KCs
Explicit probability products along paths, and chain-rule constructions, are kc3 acts wherever they occur, with a threshold, elementary independent products, one or two bare factors, sit below the engagement threshold like atomic reads, no credit and no charge, while structured constructions, product-sums, dependence-respecting path products, engage. Construal charges are exempt from the threshold, the joint-versus-conditional identification is always chargeable to kc3 because the distinction itself is the concept, whatever the mechanics beneath it. Outcome-ratio counting on an equiprobable space is kc1. Equal-weighting outcomes of unequal probability is an unsound kc1 act only when the conception of weighting unequal things equally is visible, an intended equiprobable enumeration with counting slips is execution, and mumbled reasoning takes the benefit. Decomposing an event into disjoint parts and using that a parent equals the sum of its parts is the law of total probability in joint form, a kc4 act, even with no numbers.

### 3.8 Slips
A flag never fires for a right-method-wrong-number outcome. Arithmetic slips leave KC cells at correct when the concept was soundly applied, and make question_correct wrong when the final answer misses the key.

## 4. The five knowledge components
Each KC is defined by the mathematical acts it owns. Identification acts are owned by the KC whose distinction they display, kind by kc3, direction by kc2, and they engage only when displayed, in words, in a naming, or in a failure. Silent correct reading is below threshold everywhere.
The quantitative-backing rule. On items whose gold key is a value, a KC credit requires the act to be performed quantitatively, in numbers, counts, or executed symbols, and strictly, the credited act itself must produce or operate on values, a partition credit needs the branches valued or the total formed, a restriction credit needs the restricted count or probability stated. Qualitative articulation, directions, mechanisms, partitions named as inventory, structures gestured, earns no credit on value-keyed items regardless of soundness, not engaged. Qualitative acts remain creditable where the item's own key is qualitative, the comparison items, where a verbal containment argument or a structured indeterminacy is the task itself. Unsound acts are chargeable in either form, the rule governs credit, not charges. Acts owned by no KC, elementary arithmetic, single stated-quantity reads, one or two bare independent factors, sit below the engagement threshold, no credit, no charge, their failures are slips charged to question_correct alone.

### kc1, probability foundations
Reading stated quantities as probabilities and respecting event structure. This concept covers how the participant sets up the problem's world, what the possible outcomes are, which events the question is about, and how events relate to one another, a specific combination can never be more likely than the broader event containing it. kc1 takes the item's factual furniture as given, licensed world knowledge such as a deck's composition or a die's faces is an input, never kc1 content, and ignorance of a fact is handled by the licensure and garble rules, not charged here. What kc1 owns is the construction of the sample space and events upon the given facts. An undeclared nonstandard world that is displayed, internally complete, and consistently used is graded in-world on the KC cells, with the fact-violation priced at question_correct alone, the harmonisation of the within-world doctrine, amended this session, reversing the earlier impossible-members charge, conservatively logged. kc1 charges reserve for constructions incoherent even in their own world or violating stated structure, the dropped order, misidentified referents, within-kind referent errors such as a rate attached to the wrong machine, and for worlds that would have to be reconstructed on the participant's behalf. A scope-misread that constitutes the kind change itself, a stated conditional rate read as a joint count, goes to kc3 under the demarcation, always. The sum rule lives here only in its flat form, disjoint atomic outcomes added on one level, weighted-branch summation is kc4.
- **Owns:**
  - Sample-space construction, which outcomes exist, what values a die can show, the ordered outcome set under a stated order demand
  - Event identification, which events and quantities the question is about
  - Containment and event algebra, subset ranking, a conjunction never exceeding its parent, decomposing an event into disjoint parts
  - Outcome-ratio counting on an equiprobable space, full-depth enumeration grids
  - Reading a stated split as an unconditional probability, where the item's designed task is the read
- **Failures:**
  - Invented or impossible sample-space members, an undeclared twelve-sided die
  - Dropped order under an explicit order demand
  - Containment violations, a subset ranked equal to or above its parent
  - Dependence or linkage read as equality of events
  - Manufacturing probabilities by unmotivated arithmetic on counts, reciprocals multiplied, quantities invented from a split
  - Misidentifying which events the posed comparison ranks
  - Equal-weighting outcomes of unequal probability, only when the conception is visible, an intended equiprobable enumeration with counting slips is execution, mumbled reasoning takes the benefit
- **Does not own:**
  - Forgetting members of a correctly conceived world, enumeration incompleteness, a slip
  - The atomic read of a single stated quantity inside a larger task, below threshold

### kc2, conditional probability
Conditioning as restriction of the possibility space. Learning that something is true shrinks the world to the outcomes where it holds, and probabilities are then read inside that smaller world, regardless of temporal order. The concept also covers direction, the probability of A given B and the probability of B given A restrict to different worlds and are different quantities.
- **Owns:**
  - Restriction, shrinking the world to the conditioning event and counting or measuring within it, including backward in time
  - Direction identification, keeping which-given-which distinct, naming the direction the question poses
  - Conditional values respecting a changed composition, the urn after removal
  - The insight that the two directions of a conditional restrict to different reference groups
- **Failures:**
  - Failing to restrict, valuing a conditional over the unrestricted world, or announcing restriction and never executing it
  - Asserting independence where stated structure creates dependence, refusing to update on later-revealed information
  - Computing the reversed conditional for the posed direction
  - Assembling a target by summing conditionals across different conditioning events
  - Dropping the stated condition and answering the prior
- **Does not own:**
  - The joint-for-conditional construal, kc3's distinction
  - A conditional value wrong only because an upstream misread changed the world, charged per the misread standard

### kc3, joint probability and the product rule
Building the probability that two things hold together, a marginal times the matching conditional, with dependence respected. The concept includes the kind-boundary itself, a joint, both things happening, and a conditional, one thing given the other, are different kinds of quantity, and holding them apart is as much the concept as multiplying correctly.
- **Owns:**
  - The joint-versus-conditional distinction at identification, always chargeable, threshold-exempt, credited when the which-kind decision surfaces in words
  - Chain-rule constructions, a marginal times the matching conditional, dependence respected
  - Structured product-sums, path products with unequal branch probabilities, products under stipulated independence where counting cannot reach the numbers
- **Failures:**
  - Construing a posed conditional as a joint or a posed joint as a conditional, either direction, one lost distinction, one home
  - Adding where the product rule is required
  - Multiplying unconditioned quantities as if independent when dependence is stated
- **Does not own:**
  - One or two bare independent factors, below threshold
  - Direction swaps between the two conditionals, kc2's content
  - The general symmetry assertion, the inverse flag's conception

### kc4, total probability
Assembling the overall probability of an event from its routes, weight each branch's rate by that branch's share and sum over all the branches. The concept is this branch-weighted method, not any particular quantity built with it, an evidence term reached some other valid way never engages it.
- **Owns:**
  - Weighting each branch's rate by that branch's share and summing over the partition, constructive form, building an evidence term
  - The decompositional identity, a parent equalling the sum of its disjoint parts, even with no numbers
  - Normalisation over explicitly weighted branches
- **Failures:**
  - One branch used as the total
  - Branch rates combined unweighted, or exclusive branches combined by multiplication
  - Stated unequal shares treated as equal, when the equal-weighting conception is visible
  - A Bayes evidence slot filled by a single branch's conditional where the method demanded the both-routes assembly
- **Does not own:**
  - Evidence terms reached by direct outcome enumeration or by symmetry, no partition, no engagement, their failures charged where their own acts live

### kc5, Bayesian updating
Belief revision, in two faces. The judgment face weighs the diagnosticity of evidence against a prior and reads the result as a revised belief, where finding the evidence uninformative and holding the prior is a completed sound act. The computational face divides the joint by the total evidence and reads the quotient as the posterior.
- **Owns:**
  - Judgment face, weighing the diagnosticity of evidence against a prior and reading the result as a revised belief
  - The completed null update, evidence weighed and found uninformative, prior held, and the licensure-respecting refusal to revise on unlicensed evidence
  - Computational face, dividing the joint by the total evidence and reading the quotient as the posterior
  - The Bayes identity deployed to relate quantities with prior and evidence roles correct
  - Posterior-proportional-to-likelihood reasoning under equal priors, and the insight that a small prior drags the posterior below the likelihood
- **Failures:**
  - Reporting a joint or a bare likelihood as the posterior, named the normalisation-recognition failure, offering any single unnormalised quantity, the joint, a given rate, where the posterior is asked, the machinery-absent counterpart of the kc3 conflation, distinguished from it by the one-line test, the conflation restates the target as a conjunction, the normalisation-recognition failure holds the posed conditional and answers it with the wrong object. kc4 is charged alongside where the evidence pool is likewise never conceived, one missing schema, two absent acts, each at its own concept per the required-but-absent rule, and kc4 is left unengaged where the item hands the pool over, the mammogram item's given marginal
  - Revisions contradicting the participant's own stated evaluation of the evidence
  - Revising on evidence the question does not license
  - Dividing by a fabricated or wrong-in-kind evidence term, when the division act itself is the failure, distinguished from a sound division on an upstream-corrupted input, charged upstream
- **Does not own:**
  - Assembly of the evidence term where a partition is used, kc4's method
  - Construction of the joint, kc3's act, one failure, one charge

## 5. The five misconceptions
Each misconception has a home KC and a class, listed for completeness, neither changes how you annotate.
- **conjunction (home kc1, bias class).** The conjunction fallacy. Signature conception, a specific richer scenario judged more probable than its strictly containing parent event, typically justified by plausibility or vividness of the scenario. Fires on ranking the conjunction above its parent or declaring them equally likely on scenario-weighing grounds. Does not fire when a correct ranking is given for a wrong reason.
- **inverse (home kc2, bias class).** The inverse fallacy, transposing the conditional. Signature conception, treating P(A given B) and P(B given A) as the same quantity, the symmetry rationale, they are the same thing, it goes both ways. Fires on that rationale, never on an answer value alone. A degenerate-case equality argument, for example if the test were perfect both would be certain, is wrong on correctness without a fire.
- **time_axis (home kc2, bias class).** The fallacy of the time axis. Signature conception, the later outcome carries no information about the earlier draw. The chronological refusal is its documented dress, but the fire condition is conception-level, an unconditional answer or declared impossibility justified by the claim that the later event carries no information about, cannot affect, or is independent of the earlier one, with temporal, causal, and flat-independence groundings all qualifying. A bare unconditional answer with no reasoning is unreadable, not fired.
- **denominator_neglect (home kc4, skill class).** Signature conception, the evidence total assembled from only one branch of the partition, or branch rates combined with group sizes playing no role. Fires when the second branch never enters the participant's construction of the total. Does not fire when the participant assumes branches equal in size while building both, that is a kc4 correctness failure, and does not fire when the total is not demanded by the participant's method.
- **base_rate_neglect (home kc5, bias class).** Signature conception, prior-absent reasoning, the stated base rate plays no role in a judgement or computation that required it, the evidence or likelihood carrying the whole answer. The operational test, would the participant's answer machinery change if the stated split were reversed, an inert prior fails that test and fires, a consulted prior passes it. Fires on reliability-as-verdict answers, on normalising likelihoods with the prior absent, and on judgement answers driven entirely by resemblance with the split never entering, including when the resulting answer is numerically defensible. Does not fire where using the stated quantities is the whole task and no prior weighting is demanded, and does not fire on unlicensed revision, a participant who anchors on the stated prior and revises from it on evidence the question does not license has misused updating, kc5 wrong, but has not neglected the base rate, the prior demonstrably entered, the pattern is (kc5 wrong, flag quiet).
Named errors that never fire anything, grade them wrong on the relevant KC and leave every misconception cell at its other value. Conservatism, clinging to the prior and discarding stated diagnostic evidence. The additive confusion, adding where the product rule is required, kc3. Equiprobability, assuming stated unequal groups equal, kc4. Likelihood subtraction, subtracting the false-positive rate from the sensitivity. Failure to restrict, answering about the unrestricted space, kc2.
